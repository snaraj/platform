### Added

- Give the obsync workload the `obsidian` namespace under restricted Pod Security, a hash-bound ResourceQuota and LimitRange derived from its own capacity evidence document rather than the two sites' shared one, and the `obsync-reconciler` identity with impersonation, release-reconciler and helm-reconciler Roles whose claim-lifecycle rule confers no authority over any backing volume, class, node or provisioner.
- Add the workload's own Tunnel connector to the `cloudflare-public` chart with its own instance, token Secret name, rotation revision and origin-egress leg, pointed at the in-cluster TLS proxy rather than at the application: obsync speaks plain HTTP, so a connector-matching rule on the application would hand the connector its cleartext listener, and the admitted edges are connector to proxy on the TLS port and proxy to application on the HTTP port with no connector-to-application allowance in any contributing policy.
- Make the reviewed capacity contract per namespace instead of one shared map, in `scripts/validate_repository.py` and both Conftest suites, so each namespace binds an exact five-value quota to the exact bytes of its own evidence document and a namespace with no entry has no admissible budget at all; another namespace's approved budget or evidence hash is a new refusal, proven by a negative test in each direction.
- Record the reviewable proposal for everything this repository must not write itself in `docs/design/obsync-onboarding.md`: the private WARP-routed Tunnel with no public hostname and no public DNS record, in-cluster TLS termination and the `edge.mode: none` consequence, the operator storage ceremony, and the doubled receipt closure that refuses a third identity tuple until snaraj/obsync publishes v0.1.0.
